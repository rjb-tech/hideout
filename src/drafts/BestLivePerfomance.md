## Welcome to Moon Hooch
I promise I'm not chasing a reaction besides "damn that music is cool" from my headline. I'm pretty comfortable asumming you, my dearest reader, haven't heard this album, and I'm also pretty comfortable in assuming you've never heard a band quite like Moon Hooch. Unless you know Too Many Zooz or Thundersmack, but, again, pretty comfortable assuming you haven't.

Moon Hooch got their start busking in the New York City subway tunnels. They began gaining popularity for playing energetic music that seemingly never stopped. They were able to play prolonged performances with great wind stamina and a special song queuing system. The band leader would hold one hand up high or down low with a number of fingers extended, making for songs titled "Low 2", "Number 4", and my personal favorite "Low 5".

This specific album is a masterful cocktail of saxophone technique, musicality, air bending, magic, and more. Seriously there is some crazy stuff on here. It's one of those albums that just blows me away everytime I listen to it.
