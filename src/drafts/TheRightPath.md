Let's introduce you to the bands

## Tuxedo

I found Tuxedo in the beginning of 2015, the start of my second semester in college. Their song ["Do It"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-gcfQhR_9c) came out as a single for the release cycle of their namesake debut album "Tuxedo". It was funky, retro, and bouncy enough to easily dance with. The hook is unforgettable, there is so much synth goodness going on it's hard to sit still. I didn't know it, but their identity was cemented in my mind right there from the first listen of that song. Tuxedo is so uniquely themself that it would be almost impossible to mistake them for another band.



## Leisure

Leisure is a very special artist in my life. I'm talking "wanting to run a fan club" levels of important. Favorite album of all time important. I found them in 2018. Spotify put "All Over You" from their 2016 debut album "Leisure" on my discover weekly playlist. I'm not one to speak positively of Spotify, but damn they picked a good one that week. I was immediately hooked by that song. I was still in my first vehicle at that point, and I had a pretty nice sound system that I enjoyed listening to on long drives. Night drives and sunset drives are my favorite. Anyway, I'd never heard anythign quite like "All Over You". It's a huge song. The baseline is thick like honey without being sluggish, the synths are light and wide, and the drums are consistently accentuating the deep pocket built from the very first note. I remember almost instantly looking up the whole album and exclusively listening to it for weeks. I played it on a loop while I slept. I listened to it in the shower, while driving. You name it, I was listening to Leisure doing it.

Both bands have released 4 albums, with each new album being distilled from the best parts of the last. Every album is very obviously Tuxedo without being stale or overorked. 

Both of these bands are so cool. Who else do you know that has a music video from the inside of a pinball machine?
